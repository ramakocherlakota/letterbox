How to play

Each round uses one of four modes, picked at random: Meaning → Kanji, Kanji → Meaning, Kanji → Reading or Reading → Kanji. You can choose which modes are used in Settings. Press Start for a new round. Each round has a set of words, shown a few at a time. Pick one on the left, then its match on the right. As soon as you pick both, the next word takes its place and the choices reshuffle. Every answer is listed under the board. A word you miss comes back later in the same round, so the round ends only when you have matched every word. Keyboard: number keys pick on the left, letter keys pick on the right.
